 National ID Registration & Processing Analytics (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI data analysis dashboard examining 5,000 registration records across Ethiopian regions, issuing centers, demographics, and processing timelines (2022–2026).

 Key Metrics & Insights

Overall Volume & Status: Out of 5,000 total registration records, 4,250 (85.00%) are completed, 517 (10.34%) are pending, and 233 (4.66%) were rejected.

* Regional Distribution: Oromia leads in total registrations (1,750), followed by Amhara (1,250), Addis Ababa (850), SNNPR (650), and Tigray (500), maintaining a consistent ~85% completion rate across regions.
* Issuing Center Performance:Regional registration centers handle high application volumes, with average processing times around 14–16 days.
* Demographics:Applicants have an average age of 47.17, with a nearly balanced gender split (50.4% Female, 49.6% Male) and urban/rural distribution. The Above 60 and 46–60 age brackets account for the largest share of applicants.
* Processing Bottlenecks: Most pending applications fall into the 16–30 days waiting period bucket.
